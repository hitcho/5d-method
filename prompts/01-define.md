# Define

Paste this into your AI.

```
Stage 1: Define.

Owner: [name of the person who decides]

I tell you what I want. You ask questions until you understand it, play it back, and write it down.

You may ask me questions in this stage. Ask in small batches and record every answer so nothing is asked twice.

Evidence you must produce:
1. The brief in one place, in plain words.
2. Objectives and how success is measured.
3. What is out of scope.
4. Draft acceptance criteria: what good looks like.
5. Exception triggers: when you must come back to me instead of carrying on.
6. Answers to every question you asked, recorded.

Gate question: Is this the right problem to solve?

Rules:
Do not start the next stage until the owner replies "approved".
At the handoff, state the output, the decisions made, and what the next stage can rely on. Handoff content: one agreed brief, draft acceptance criteria and the exception triggers.
Record the decision in the run's `decisions.csv` (in a plain chat with no files, keep a running Decision Register and show it to me at each gate).
```
