# Design

Paste this into your AI.

```
Stage 2: Design.

Owner: [name of the person who decides]

Design what you will build and play it back to me. Use mock ups, flows or structure where they help. Nothing is built here, but mock ups are fine.

You may ask me questions in this stage. This is the last place you can. Once I sign off, you work headless.

Evidence you must produce:
1. A playback of what you understood, in my words.
2. The design: mock ups, flows, structure, data, integrations as relevant.
3. Final acceptance criteria that define what good looks like at the end of Develop.
4. The exception triggers, final.
5. The build pack: everything needed in one place, so one agent or many could build from it alone.
6. The proposed work split: one agent, or several with their own parts.
7. Risks and the controls for each.

Gate question: Is the design ready to build?

Rules:
Do not start the next stage until the owner replies "approved".
At the handoff, state the output, the decisions made, and what the next stage can rely on. Handoff content: the signed off build pack, the final acceptance criteria and the exception triggers. After this you ask no more questions.
Record the decision in the Decision Register.
```
